# BSR-1 — Sales Enablement Pack

## What BSR-1 is
A self-service AI assistant that answers a business's customers using the knowledge supplied by that business. The customer receives two separate links: a public assistant link and a private customer control-panel link.

## Customer problem
Businesses repeatedly answer the same questions, miss requests outside working hours, and lack simple visibility into how customers use their support channels.

## Core value
- Gives customers instant responses from the business's own approved knowledge.
- Reduces repetitive manual answering.
- Captures callback and human-help requests instead of losing them.
- Lets the buyer manage branding, knowledge and usage without depending on BASEERA every day.
- Provides usage and customer-response analytics that demonstrate product value.

## Product capabilities
- Branded AI chat assistant.
- Customer-controlled assistant name, logo, primary color, welcome message, active language, working hours, contact details and social links.
- Arabic or English active language per customer.
- Knowledge by question/answer plus PDF, Word and Excel upload.
- Public assistant link + iframe embed + website widget.
- Callback request and human-help request with name + phone or email + reason.
- Email notification to the subscribed business for contact/help requests.
- End-of-chat question: "Was your request resolved?" Yes/No + optional note.
- Subscription dates, AI used/remaining and clear renew/upgrade actions.
- AI usage alerts at 20%, 10%, 5% remaining.
- 3-day grace period after expiry, then AI stops while the dashboard stays available.
- Owner + one additional dashboard user.
- 12-month conversation/customer-data retention policy.

## Package differences
### Essential
- 10,000 AI responses/month
- Up to 10 knowledge files
- Analytics: users, conversations, AI usage
- Email support

### Professional
- 50,000 AI responses/month
- Up to 50 knowledge files
- Essential analytics + resolution rate + callback requests + human-help requests
- Priority email support

### Enterprise
- 200,000 AI responses/month
- Up to 200 knowledge files
- Professional analytics + top questions + peak usage time + usage trend + returning-user rate
- Dedicated support
- Custom-domain capability may be introduced later under controlled rollout

## Ideal customers
Need-first targeting. Any business or individual with repeated customer questions, a meaningful volume of enquiries, or a need to capture after-hours/customer-service requests. V1 is especially easy for small and medium businesses but is not limited to them.

## Sales conversation structure
Problem → impact → BSR-1 fit → concrete value → suitable package → next step.

## Claims Sales may safely make
- Customer manages its own knowledge and assistant settings.
- The assistant answers from customer-provided knowledge and is designed not to invent unsupported facts.
- AI quota is package-controlled and hard-capped.
- The product supports direct link, embed and website widget.
- Callback/help requests can be captured and emailed to the business.

## Claims Sales must not make without evidence/approval
- Guaranteed cost savings or revenue increase.
- Guaranteed answer accuracy percentage.
- Unlimited AI usage.
- Features not present in the purchased package.
- Any unapproved price, discount, SLA or legal/compliance claim.

## Pricing rule
Sales does not create product prices. Only Finance-recommended and Founder-approved commercial prices may be quoted.
