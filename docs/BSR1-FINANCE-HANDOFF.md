# BSR-1 — Finance Pricing Handoff

Status: Prepared by Product & Technology for Finance Pricing Review after Founder Trial readiness.

## Product model
Self-service AI customer-response assistant. Customer configures knowledge, branding, language, hours, contact information and installation. BASEERA support is on-request rather than daily managed operations.

## Package operating limits — not prices
| Package | AI responses / month | Knowledge files | Analytics | Support |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Essential | 10,000 | 10 | Basic: users, conversations, AI usage | Email |
| Professional | 50,000 | 50 | Essential + resolution rate + callbacks + help requests | Priority email |
| Enterprise | 200,000 | 200 | Professional + top questions + peak time + trend + returning users | Dedicated support |

All commercial prices, setup fees, discounts and future pricing changes are owned by Finance and subject to Founder final pricing approval. Product & Technology must not publish or invent prices.

## Cost inputs Finance should evaluate
- AI inference cost by package usage pattern and model mix.
- Hosting/serverless execution.
- Database/storage and 12-month retention cost.
- Knowledge document parsing for PDF/Word/Excel.
- Email notification cost.
- Support cost-to-serve by package.
- Monitoring, maintenance and incident allowance.
- Expected inactive/unused allowance and peak usage risk.
- Custom domain support for higher packages when introduced.

## Commercial behavior relevant to pricing
- Manual activation/renewal/upgrade in V1; architecture prepared for online payment later.
- 3-day grace period after subscription end, then AI stops; dashboard remains available for renewal.
- Hard AI cap. Assistant stops AI responses when quota is exhausted.
- Client alerts at 20%, 10% and 5% AI remaining.
- Owner + one additional dashboard user.

## Finance output required
Pricing Recommendation with proposed setup/subscription price per package, expected cost, margin, break-even where useful, market comparison supplied by Market Intelligence, and rationale. Final commercial price requires Founder approval before Sales uses it.
